About Us

Who We Are

Founded by Software Engineering students, HIT Coding Club is open to every student at the institute, whatever their programme.

Vision

A generation of HIT graduates who build world-class software for Africa.

Mission

To provide a supportive space for learning, collaboration and innovation in software development.

Objectives

  1. Teach practical programming skills through workshops.
  2. Run team projects that solve local problems.
  3. Connect students with mentors and industry.
  4. Prepare members for hackathons and competitions.
